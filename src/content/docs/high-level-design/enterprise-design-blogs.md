# Enterprise Engineering Blogs & Patterns

## 📄 Foundational White Papers

### Databases & Storage
* **[Amazon Aurora](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs245/readings/aurora.pdf)**: How Amazon provides high-availability and performance.
* **[BigTable](https://static.googleusercontent.com/media/research.google.com/en//archive/bigtable-osdi06.pdf)**: The design principles behind a distributed storage system.
* **[Cassandra](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/projects/ladis2009/papers/lakshman-ladis2009.pdf)**: A look at the design and architecture of a distributed NoSQL database.
* **[Dynamo - Amazon’s Highly Available Key Value Store](https://www.allthingsdistributed.com/files/amazon-dynamo-sosp2007.pdf)**: The foundation of Amazon's highly available key-value store.
* **[FoundationDB](https://www.foundationdb.org/files/fdb-paper.pdf)**: A look at the mechanics of a distributed database.
* **[Google File System (GFS)](https://static.googleusercontent.com/media/research.google.com/en//archive/gfs-sosp2003.pdf)**: Insights into a highly scalable file system.
* **[Monarch](https://storage.googleapis.com/pub-tools-public-publication-data/pdf/d84ab6c93881af998de877d0070a706de7bec6d8.pdf)**: Architecture of Google’s in-memory time series database.
* **[MyRocks](https://www.vldb.org/pvldb/vol13/p3217-matsunobu.pdf)**: LSM-Tree database storage engine.
* **[Scaling Memcached at Facebook](https://research.facebook.com/file/839620310074473/scaling-memcache-at-facebook.pdf)**: A look at the complexities of caching at scale.
* **[Spanner](https://static.googleusercontent.com/media/research.google.com/en//archive/spanner-osdi2012.pdf)**: Design and architecture of Google’s globally distributed database.
* **[Vector Databases](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.11703.pdf)**: A comprehensive survey on vector databases.

### Distributed Systems & Infrastructure
* **[Borg - Large Scale Cluster Management](https://storage.googleapis.com/pub-tools-public-publication-data/pdf/43438.pdf)**: Cluster management at Google.
* **[Dapper](https://static.googleusercontent.com/media/research.google.com/en//archive/papers/dapper-2010-1.pdf)**: Insights into Google’s distributed systems tracing infrastructure.
* **[Flink](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/308993790_Apache_Flink_Stream_and_Batch_Processing_in_a_Single_Engine)**: A detailed look at the unified architecture of stream and batch processing.
* **[Kafka](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/Kafka.pdf)**: Internals of the distributed messaging platform.
* **[MapReduce](https://storage.googleapis.com/pub-tools-public-publication-data/pdf/16cb30b4b92fd4989b8619a61752a2387c6dd474.pdf)**: A detailed look at parallel processing of massive volumes of data.
* **[Raft Consensus Algorithm](https://raft.github.io/raft.pdf)**: The understandable consensus algorithm.
* **[Shard Manager](https://dl.acm.org/doi/pdf/10.1145/3477132.3483546)**: Understanding the generic shard management framework.
* **[Thrift](https://thrift.apache.org/static/files/thrift-20070401.pdf)**: Explore the design choices behind Facebook’s code-generation tool.
* **[Time Clocks and Ordering of Events](https://lamport.azurewebsites.net/pubs/time-clocks.pdf)**: The foundational paper explaining time and event ordering in a distributed system.
* **[Zanzibar](https://storage.googleapis.com/pub-tools-public-publication-data/pdf/10683a8987dbf0c6d4edcafb9b4f05cc9de5974a.pdf)**: Global system for managing access control lists at Google.

### AI, Algorithms & Theory
* **[Attention Is All You Need](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762)**: The introduction to the Transformer deep learning architecture.
* **[Bitcoin](https://bitcoin.org/bitcoin.pdf)**: The ground-breaking introduction to the peer-to-peer electronic cash system.
* **[GoTo Considered Harmful](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~storm/teaching/reader/Dijkstra68.pdf)**: Edgar Dijkstra's classic critique on program structure.
* **[WTF - Who to Follow Service at Twitter](https://web.stanford.edu/~rezab/papers/wtf_overview.pdf)**: Twitter’s user recommendation system.
